View CV eyalbaruch@technion.ac.il

Research Directions

Lightweight & Deployable Structures

Dynamics, modeling, scaling, and experiments for ultralight and foldable structural systems.

Smart Structures

Structures that integrate sensing, damping, actuation, and control into the mechanical design.

Nonlinear Dynamics & Control

Model-based analysis and control of flexible and nonlinear mechanical systems.

Additive Manufacturing

Using advanced manufacturing to create new structural architectures with tailored dynamic behavior.

Origami & Robotic Structures

Foldable mechanisms and robotic structures whose geometry is used to control motion and functionality.

Dynamic Scaling & Model Updating

Data-driven methods for relating experiments, simulations, and structures across different scales.
